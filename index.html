UB Core Values

The University of Batangas promotes core values and attributes that help students develop not only academically but also personally and professionally. These values guide students in becoming responsible, capable, and meaningful members of society. As a BS Information Technology student, these values can also be connected to the skills and responsibilities needed in the field of Information Technology.

Wisdom

Connection to Information Technology

Wisdom is important in Information Technology because IT students need to think carefully before making decisions. In programming, a small mistake can affect the entire system, so understanding the problem first is very important. Wisdom also helps an IT student choose the right technology and solution for a specific situation. It encourages students to use information responsibly instead of simply following what others are doing. As a future IT professional, wisdom can help me create solutions that are useful, safe, and beneficial to other people.

Service

Connection to Information Technology

Service can be connected to Information Technology because technology is created to help people solve problems and make tasks easier. IT students can use their skills to create websites, applications, and systems that can benefit others. Helping classmates understand technology is also a simple example of service. An IT professional should not only focus on technical skills but also consider the needs of the users. Through service, I can use my knowledge in Information Technology to help people and contribute to the community.

Builder

Connection to Information Technology

Being a Builder is strongly connected to Information Technology because IT professionals create systems, websites, programs, and other digital solutions. Building something in IT requires creativity, patience, and continuous improvement. An IT student must be willing to learn from mistakes while developing a project. Every line of code can contribute to creating a useful and functional product. As a future IT professional, I can become a Builder by creating technology that can solve real problems.

Communicator

Connection to Information Technology

Communication is important in Information Technology because IT professionals often work with different people and teams. An IT student needs to explain technical ideas in a simple and understandable way. Good communication also helps developers understand what users actually need from a system. When working on group projects, clear communication can prevent mistakes and misunderstandings. By becoming a good Communicator, I can work better with users, classmates, and future IT professionals.

Global Citizen

Connection to Information Technology

Being a Global Citizen is connected to Information Technology because technology connects people from different countries and cultures. The internet allows information and ideas to be shared around the world in only a short amount of time. An IT student should understand that technology can affect people beyond their local community. It is also important to respect different cultures and use digital platforms responsibly. As a future IT professional, I can use technology to connect with others and contribute to solutions that can help people around the world.

Lifelong Learner

Connection to Information Technology

Lifelong learning is very important in Information Technology because technology continues to change over time. New programming languages, tools, applications, and technologies are constantly being developed. Because of this, an IT student cannot stop learning after graduating from college. Continuous learning helps IT professionals improve their skills and stay updated with new developments. As a future BSIT graduate, I want to continue learning so I can adapt to changes and become a better IT professional.
