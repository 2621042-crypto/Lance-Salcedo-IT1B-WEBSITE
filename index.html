UB Core Values

01 / CORE VALUE

Faith in God

Meaning

Faith in God is the foundation of a UBian's spiritual life. It is the deep trust and belief in a higher power that guides moral decisions, fosters humility, and provides inner strength during challenges.

Connection to IT

Translates into ethical technology development. As we build algorithms and manage user data, a spiritual foundation reminds us that technology must serve humanity's well-being and uphold truth.

02 / CORE VALUE

Love of Wisdom

Meaning

Love of Wisdom signifies the relentless pursuit of knowledge, critical thinking, and intellectual excellence. It urges UBians to look beyond the surface, question assumptions, and continuously seek truth.

Connection to IT

Information Technology evolves daily. A love of wisdom drives IT professionals to engage in continuous learning, mastering new frameworks, security protocols, system architectures, and logical reasoning.

03 / CORE VALUE

Service to Fellowmen

Meaning

Service to Fellowmen defines a UBian's purpose as being a helper, builder, and defender of others. It means using one's talents and skills to uplift communities and address societal needs.

Connection to IT

IT is a tool for social good. Whether building civic-tech platforms, creating assistive medical systems, or developing technology to simplify daily human life, IT serves to solve real-world problems.

UB Attributes (BEST)

01 / BEST ATTRIBUTE

Builder and Innovator of Knowledge

Meaning

This attribute encourages UBians to create new ideas, construct functional products, and pioneer innovative solutions instead of merely consuming existing knowledge.

Connection to IT

In IT, innovation is the default state. Students must transition from being coders into software engineers who design new algorithms, architect databases, and build original digital tools.

02 / BEST ATTRIBUTE

Efficient Professional & Effective Communicator

Meaning

UBians are trained to carry themselves with professional excellence and convey ideas clearly, persuasively, and with empathy across different mediums.

Connection to IT

An IT professional cannot work in a silo. We must translate complex technical jargon into clear business requirements for clients, write clean documentation, and collaborate in agile teams.

03 / BEST ATTRIBUTE

Social, Moral, and Global-minded Citizen

Meaning

Demands that UBians recognize their place in the global community while maintaining strict moral integrity and advocating for social justice locally.

Connection to IT

IT systems cross borders instantly. As global citizens, we consider the ethical implications of data mining, respect global privacy laws (such as GDPR), and design secure digital ecosystems.

04 / BEST ATTRIBUTE

Transformed Lifelong Learner

Meaning

Emphasizes a perpetual process of self-improvement, adaptation, and transformation in response to an ever-changing world.

Connection to IT

A programming language or framework popular today may be replaced tomorrow. An IT graduate must possess the meta-skill of "learning how to learn" to view change as an opportunity to adapt.
